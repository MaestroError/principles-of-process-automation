# Process Specification: AI research-record collection

**Kind:** Prescriptive, illustrative. **Version:** 1.
**Boundary:** From receipt of an approved collection charter and record set to a source-supported collection at the charter's deadline. All operators, escalation recipients, reviewing roles, and the bounded process owner inside this boundary are AI agents. No human approval or human escalation is assumed.
**Authority source:** A pre-existing collection charter supplies scope, permitted sources, evidence rules, processing deadline, access permissions, and revision limits. Charter creation and organizational accountability are outside this process; that boundary does not imply their absence.

## Objective

Assess records and produce an accepted collection whose claims have accessible, attributable source evidence. Exclude unrelated records and records whose evidence cannot support the proposed claim. Never mark unsupported claims verified, expand access permissions, or change the charter's evidence standard.

Coverage may decrease to preserve evidential quality. Success is a collection with accepted records and a separate disposition list for excluded or unresolved records. It is not necessary to accept every input. The collection closes at the charter deadline, even if some records remain unresolved.

## Occurrence

The charter and intake set become available. Required readiness includes accessible sources and provenance, storage for evidence and dispositions, and permissions matching assigned roles. The deadline must leave enough time for the required closeout checks; otherwise retain the intake as pending and report the missing readiness evidence in the disposition record.

Reassess source access and permission validity before acceptance. If either is lost, suspend affected records. Resume only after restoration within the processing window. At the deadline, unresolved records remain outside the accepted collection and receive a recorded reason.

## Order

| Activity | Dependency | Role and decision rights |
|---|---|---|
| Establish provenance | Accessible source and source identity | Record assessor may accept or reject provenance against charter criteria. |
| Assess claim support | Provenance established | Record assessor applies evidence criteria and cites source spans. |
| Resolve methodological ambiguity | Specialist acknowledges transfer | Specialist assessor may resolve within evidence rules; may not lower the standard. |
| Resolve permitted procedure questions | Steward acknowledges transfer | Collection steward may clarify or revise procedure within charter limits. |
| Accept record | Evidence criteria satisfied, permissions valid | Record assessor writes accepted record and decision evidence. |
| Close collection | Deadline reached; dispositions available | Collection steward checks that accepted and unresolved sets are distinguished. |

Independent records may proceed concurrently. Shared source-access capacity follows the charter's request budget. Record identity prevents conflicting accepted versions: a conflicting update moves the record to pending reconciliation before acceptance. No source-dependent decision uses an unverified replacement source.

## Option

Use a charter-permitted alternative source only after verifying its provenance; record the substitution. If every route depends on the same unavailable source, mark that limitation rather than claiming an independent fallback.

**Methodology escalation:** Ambiguous evidence goes to the specialist assessor. Transfer the record identity, source spans, criteria applied, checks performed, current state, and specific question. The specialist has source-reading and decision-record permissions and must acknowledge within one assessment cycle or before the collection deadline, whichever comes first. The charter defines an assessment cycle as one completed review pass, with a ten-minute elapsed-time limit. On acknowledgment, responsibility for the methodology decision transfers; the original assessor cannot accept the record until that decision returns.

**Procedure escalation:** A question outside the specialist's decision rights goes to the collection steward, using the same transfer contents and acknowledgment bound. The steward is the recipient only for this class. Its process-owner role does not make it the default destination for all uncertain records.

If a recipient is unavailable, cannot acknowledge within the bound, or cannot resolve the question before closeout, retain the record as pending and exclude it from the accepted collection. A returned decision that still lacks required support remains unresolved; do not cycle it through the same agents indefinitely. A requested charter change is outside authority: record it as a proposal for the charter's governing authority without claiming it was approved or delivered.

The accepted and unresolved sets must be preserved before activity ends. If storage is unavailable, do not claim successful closeout; retain the in-progress state if possible and record the incomplete outcome through the charter's available evidence surface. Storage availability is an implementation prerequisite, not guaranteed by these words.

## Observation

Each record retains provenance, cited source spans, criteria applied, decision, substitutions, transferred actions and state, acknowledgments, and unresolved reason. Reviewing roles can inspect this evidence using charter-granted read access.

At closeout the collection steward checks a charter-defined sample and all unresolved dispositions against the evidence standard. This is a finite collection: completion evidence and closeout review apply; continuous accumulated/trend monitoring is not required. Material access or source changes trigger reassessment during the collection window.

The steward may revise assessment order or clarification guidance within the charter's revision limits, identify the new version, and notify affected assessor roles before further activity uses it. It cannot lower evidence criteria or enlarge scope. Findings outside those limits remain proposals, not enacted changes.

## Roles and governance

| Role | Assignment | Required capability and authority |
|---|---|---|
| Activity operator | Record assessor AI | Source access, evidence assessment, bounded record updates. |
| Escalation recipient | Specialist assessor AI | Methodological review and decision recording within fixed evidence rules. |
| Process owner; recipient for procedure questions | Collection steward AI | Closeout evaluation and bounded procedure revision, not charter amendment. |

One entity fills two explicitly distinct roles. The charter identifies the governing authority able to amend or withdraw these assignments and the surrounding organizational accountability. Functional AI ownership does not transfer or settle that accountability. The specification imposes no human-only role inside its boundary.

Objects include the charter, records, source spans, accepted set, pending/excluded dispositions, and revision identifiers. Resources include source access, permission scopes, assessment capacity, storage, communication, and the processing window. A proposed implementation must demonstrate these capabilities and acknowledgment mechanisms; the specification does not make agents interchangeable.
