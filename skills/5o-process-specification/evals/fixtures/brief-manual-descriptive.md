# Current practice: Paper library lending desk

Document current practice only; do not redesign it. Clerks and the lending supervisor are people. All records are paper. A clerk receives a member's request, checks the membership card and item category, records a due date in the loan register, and releases eligible items. Reference-only items are never lent. Clerks share the register in arrival order. The library services manager owns the desk procedure and may revise it within lending policy; it is a different person from the lending supervisor.

Disputed eligibility goes to the supervisor with the item record and membership card. The supervisor sometimes leaves before the desk closes. Nobody knows how acknowledgment is recorded or what clerks actually do while waiting; this is a fact to investigate, not an agreed new rule. The manager checks overdue entries weekly. We do not know how long records are retained. Do not introduce software or turn this into an automation proposal.
