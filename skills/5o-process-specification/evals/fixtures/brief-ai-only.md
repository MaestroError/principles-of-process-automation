# Process Brief: AI-only research collection

Kind: Prescriptive. All roles inside this process are AI agents: record assessor, specialist assessor, and collection steward. The steward owns the process and may revise assessment order and clarification guidance within charter v1, but cannot alter scope, evidence rules, access permissions, or the deadline. Charter v1 supplies authority; its governing organization retains wider accountability outside this boundary. Do not add human review or human escalation.

The outcome is a collection of source-supported records by 18:00 UTC on the charter date, plus excluded/pending dispositions. Coverage may be sacrificed to evidence quality. Intake requires accessible source provenance, writable record storage, permissions matching the role, and an open processing window. Recheck source access and permissions before acceptance. Independent records can run concurrently within the charter's budget. Provenance precedes support assessment; support precedes acceptance; conflicting versions remain pending until reconciled.

Methodological uncertainty goes to the specialist assessor, not automatically to the steward. The specialist can inspect sources and resolve within the fixed evidence standard. Transfer record id, source spans, criteria/checks, actions, current state, and requested decision. It must acknowledge within ten minutes or before 18:00, whichever comes first. Responsibility for that decision transfers on acknowledgment. Unavailable/unresolved cases remain pending outside the accepted set by closeout; do not cycle them repeatedly. Only procedure-policy questions go to the steward under the same contract. Source substitutes must be charter-permitted and provenance-checked. A source outage may disable every source-dependent route; do not claim an independent fallback.

Record sources, decisions, substitutions, transfers, acknowledgments, and unresolved reasons. At closeout the steward checks all unresolved dispositions and a charter-defined sample of accepted records, then identifies any authorized procedure revision by version and notifies assessors. This is a finite collection, so closeout review applies; continuous trend monitoring is not required. Tools and agent implementation choices are outside this specification.
